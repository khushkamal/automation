## 📌 Slide 8: WhatsApp Outreach Tool Daily SOP
1. **Step 1:** Dashboard ke **WhatsApp Outreach Center** tab par jayein aur apne WhatsApp se QR scan karein.
2. **Step 2 (Automated Blast):** Status `🟢 System Connected` aate hi click karein **`🚀 Start 1-Click Automated Campaign`** (Auto anti-ban delay 6–10s ke sath pitches dispatch honge).
3. **Step 3 (Custom Numbers):** Custom numbers paste karein ya 1-click se Instagram / Maps leads load karke blast karein.
4. **Step 4 (Manual Pitch):** Leads table me kisi bhi lead ke aage **"💬 View & Send Pitch"** click karke individual message send karein.
